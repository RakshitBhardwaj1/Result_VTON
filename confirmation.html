Photo Upload Guidelines

Important: Images should be clear and face should be clearly visible for the best virtual try-on experience.
Please ensure your photos meet these requirements:
  • Good lighting conditions (avoid dark or blurry photos)
  • Face and full body should be clearly visible
  • Stand straight with arms slightly away from body
  • Wear fitted clothing (avoid loose or flowing garments)
  • Plain or uncluttered background
  • Remove accessories like hats, sunglasses, or bulky jewelry
